My Story

There's a moment most of us know: the moment you realize you've stopped, and you're not sure how to start again.

For me, that moment came after time away from school and out of the workforce after having my child. Starting again didn't look like a big leap. It looked like a certificate in computer programming. It was my first big achievement in a long time, and it taught me something I still carry: a break is not a failure. It's a place to begin again.

From there, I kept building. I earned a full-stack web development certificate and discovered that I learn best by creating something real. My capstone was a web application for the Syracuse City School District's summer reading program, the same district I grew up in, and the same district I work for today.

Now I spend my days in accounts payable, where one wrong number can hold up a payment. That work taught me that numbers are never just numbers. Behind every figure is a person, a business, or a decision waiting to be made.

That's the idea that drives me: data matters most when people understand it. My goal is to use data to answer real questions, and to help people see why the answers matter and how to use them.

I'm still building, as a student, a single parent, and a developer. If you're starting again too, I hope this is a reminder that where you begin doesn't decide where you go.

What I Value
