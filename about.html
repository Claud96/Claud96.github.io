My Story

Returning to school after time away taught me that a break is not a failure. It gave me a clearer sense of purpose and stronger discipline. Earning my certificate in computer programming was my first big achievement after being out of the workforce for some time.

I earned a full-stack web development certificate and built a web app for the Syracuse City School District's summer reading program, the same district I grew up in and work for today. In accounts payable, I've learned that behind every figure is a person or a decision waiting to be made.

That's what drives me: data matters most when people understand it. I'm building a career using data to answer real questions and help people see why the answers matter.

What I Value

Balance

Work should support a full life, not replace it. I look for work I'm passionate about, so it rarely feels like just a job.

People First

Every number and every process exists to serve people. I want to work in an envirnment where people are treated as humans, not resources.

How I Show Up
